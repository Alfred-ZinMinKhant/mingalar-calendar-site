Mingalar Calendar: MM & Thai

One calendar for Myanmar and Thai holidays, made for people from Myanmar living or working in Thailand.

What it does

  • Shows the Myanmar calendar (month, moon phase, Sabbath days) next to the Gregorian date, with Myanmar Era and Thai Buddhist Era years.
  • Myanmar holidays and Thai holidays on the same month view. Switch either one off.
  • A home screen widget with today's date, today's holidays and the next holiday.
  • Available in English, Burmese and Thai.
  • Works offline. Holiday updates arrive quietly when you are online.
  • Free, with ads. No account.

About the dates

Myanmar calendar dates are calculated with the open algorithm by Yan Naing Aye (yan9a/mmcal, MIT license). Holiday dates are still being checked against official announcements, and the app marks holidays it has not confirmed. Please check an official source before you plan around a date.
